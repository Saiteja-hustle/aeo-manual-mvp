# Side page template (Articles, Discussions, Listings, Competitors)

## Why we are doing this
Plain reason tied to evidence from the audit. One short paragraph.

## How to do it
1. Numbered, concrete steps someone can follow without asking.
2. Include length targets, structure, and what to avoid.
3. Say how to disclose (we make [product]).

## Evidence
What the cited pages or competitors do, with numbers (word counts, FAQ counts, positions, dates).

## What to check afterwards
Which prompt to re-run, how many runs, which day, what to log (ship date, GSC position before and after).

## AI prompt for Claude
```
Task, product facts to use, structure, tone, constraints.
Use only facts I paste. Mark anything unverified [VERIFY].
Disclose that we make [product]. Do not copy other sites' wording.
Give 2 variants for outreach. No names.
Facts: [paste]
```

For Competitors rows use: What we observed / Why it gets in / What we can learn / AI prompt.
For skipped rows: say why it is skipped and what to do instead.
